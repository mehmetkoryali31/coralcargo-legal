Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026 · Version 2

For players in Türkiye, the KVKK Information Notice is section 13 of this policy.

1. Controller

CORAL CARGO is developed and published by Mohammed I Nooruldeen Nooruldeen (an individual). Controller: Mohammed I Nooruldeen Nooruldeen. Contact: mehmetkoryali31@gmail.com

2. No account, nothing sent

There is no account and no sign-in. The game does not ask for your identity, phone number, e-mail address, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera. The game does not send your data to us: it has no analytics, no crash reporting and no cloud save. The game works offline; only the ads described in section 4 use the internet, through Google's own software.

3. Stored only on this device

The following is stored only on this device and is never sent to us:

  • Company: the company name you type, the country and home airport you choose, and when the company was founded.
  • Game: your progress and the levels you have flown, your cash and XP, your fleet, upgrades and chosen aircraft, your company ledger and a flight you have not finished yet.
  • Settings: your language and the flight difficulty.
  • Age group: on first launch you are asked your month and year of birth. Your birth date is not stored; only your age group (under 13, 13–15, 16–17, 18 and over) and the month it next changes are kept, and the group updates itself over time.
  • A record about these documents: which version you accepted or saw, when and in which language, and later your answers to optional permissions.

The company name is only shown in the game; it is never sent anywhere.

4. Ads

CORAL CARGO is free and supported by ads. Ads appear only in these places:

  • After a delivery: at most one full-screen ad when you tap Continue on the waybill, and not more often than once every few minutes.
  • After buying an aircraft: at most one full-screen ad when you close the purchase message.
  • On the delivery waybill, an optional rewarded ad: if you choose to watch it to the end, you receive 25% of that delivery's net profit as a bonus, once per delivered level.
  • No ads are shown during a flight, on loading screens or on the welcome screen. If no ad is available, nothing changes in the game.

Ads are served by Google (Google AdMob, Google Mobile Ads SDK). To show ads, measure them and prevent abuse, Google processes: the advertising identifier (on Android the advertising ID; on iPhone the IDFA only with your permission), your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device and app information (device model, operating system, language, app version), your interactions with ads (views, taps, watch time) and diagnostic information of the ad service. This data goes directly from Google's own software to Google, not from the game; it does not reach us as personal data. Google processes it under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

  • Under 13: the ad software is never started and no ads are shown.
  • 13–17: ads are never personalised, Google is told you are a teen user, and the iPhone tracking question is not asked.
  • 18 and over: ads are personalised only with your permission (Google's consent form in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland; elsewhere our own question, asked once; on iPhone also Apple's tracking permission). If you do not give permission, the game works exactly the same.
  • Ads are shown only in countries whose rules have been checked. Your country is taken from your store account, on Android from your SIM card and mobile network, and from Google's region information; no location permission is asked and this information does not leave the device.
  • In Türkiye: rewarded ads are shown without personalisation to everyone aged 13 or over. Personalised ads and the full-screen ads you did not ask for are shown only if you are 18 or older and tap "Allow" on the separate question; that answer is also your explicit consent to the transfer of ad data abroad to Google (USA) (KVKK Art. 5/1, 9/6-a). Details are in section 13.
  • Rewarded ads are always optional; progress in the game is never locked behind an ad.

5. Age

The game is suitable for all ages. Your age group is used only to apply the ad rules in section 4. The age group entered on this device can later only be lowered, never raised.

6. What is not in the game

The game has no analytics or crash reporting system of our own, no cloud save, no chat or messaging, no notifications and no real-money purchases.

7. App stores

When you download the game from the App Store or Google Play, these stores process their own information for downloads and updates under their own privacy policies.

8. System backups

If your device's system backup is on (iCloud or computer backup on iPhone, Google account backup on Android), your game data may stay in these backups and come back when the game is reinstalled. We do not hold these backups; you can manage them in your device and account settings.

9. International transfers

Google may process ad data outside your country, including in the United States. Game data on your device is not transferred anywhere. Details for players in Türkiye are in section 13.

10. Deletion

Settings > Start a new company deletes your company and all progress from this device. Removing the game from your device deletes all of its data, including your settings and the record about these documents. As nothing is sent to us, we hold no data about you that could be deleted.

11. Your rights

Depending on the law of your country you have rights such as access, correction, deletion, objection and withdrawing your permission. Because your data is only on your device, you can use most of them in the game itself. For other requests: mehmetkoryali31@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to the data protection authority of your country.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, the new text is shown to you when you open the game and the effective date is updated. Showing information does not mean consent; optional permissions are always asked separately.

13. KVKK Information Notice for players in Türkiye

This section is the information notice under Article 10 of Turkish Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK); it is given for information and is not a consent text.

13.1 Controller

Controller: Mohammed I Nooruldeen Nooruldeen (an individual), who develops and publishes CORAL CARGO. Contact: mehmetkoryali31@gmail.com

13.2 Data processed and how it is collected

a) Game data that stays only on your device: company name, the country and home airport you chose, progress, cash and XP, fleet and upgrades, settings, your age group (your birth date is not stored) and the record about these documents. You enter it in the game or it is created as you play. It is not sent to us and the controller has no access to it.

b) Ad data (players aged 13 or over whose age is known): the data listed in section 4, collected automatically by Google's ad software when an ad is shown. If your age is unknown or you are under 13, the ad software is never started and nothing is sent to Google. Google receives only a "non-personalised" flag and, for ages 13–17, a "teen user" flag, never your birth date or age.

13.3 Purposes

Running the game on your device and keeping your progress and settings; showing ads so the game can stay free, measuring ads and preventing ad fraud; giving the rewarded-ad bonus you ask for; showing personalised ads and the full-screen ads you did not ask for only with your explicit consent.

13.4 Legal grounds

Game data on your device: being directly related to the performance of the contract made with you for using the game (KVKK Art. 5/2-c). Rewarded ads that you ask for to get the bonus, to the extent necessary to give it (Art. 5/2-c); measuring ads and preventing abuse, the controller's legitimate interest without harming your fundamental rights and freedoms (Art. 5/2-f); personalised ads and the full-screen ads you did not ask for (after a delivery, after a purchase), only for players aged 18 or over, on your explicit consent (Art. 5/1). Giving consent is optional; without it the game stays exactly the same.

13.5 Transfers abroad

Ad data is processed by Google LLC (USA) and its affiliates outside Türkiye, including in the USA. There is no adequacy decision for the USA and Google does not offer the standard contract provided for by the KVKK, so the transfer rests on KVKK Art. 9/6: for rewarded ads, to the extent necessary to give the bonus you asked for (Art. 9/6-b); for personalised ads and the full-screen ads you did not ask for, on your explicit consent given after being informed of the possible risks (Art. 9/6-a). Possible risks: your data may be less protected than in Türkiye, the authorities of that country may access it under their own law, and exercising your rights there may be harder. Game data on your device is not transferred anywhere.

13.6 Retention

Data on your device stays there until you delete it or remove the game. We keep no data about you. Google keeps ad data for a limited time under its own rules: https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention

13.7 Withdrawing consent

You can withdraw or give again your explicit consent at any time, as easily as you gave it, in Settings > Legal and privacy. After withdrawal, personalised ads and the full-screen ads you did not ask for stop; processing before the withdrawal is not affected.

13.8 Your rights (KVKK Art. 11)

You have the right to learn whether your personal data is processed, to request information if it is, to learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used for that purpose, to know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom it is transferred, to request correction if it is incomplete or wrong, to request deletion or destruction where the conditions are met and notification of these actions to those third parties, to object to a result against you arising solely from automated analysis, and to claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing. Send your requests by e-mail to mehmetkoryali31@gmail.com, stating your name, contact details and your request. Requests are concluded free of charge within 30 days at the latest. If your request is rejected, the answer is insufficient or there is no answer in time, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Board.
